# Project Naming Ideas

Here are some ideas for the project name, blending the concepts of Kubernetes, AI, Cinema, and Generation.

**Favorites:**
*   **Kine-matic**
*   **Genie-CTL**
*   **Kube-Gem**

---

### Short, Punchy & Professional
*   **Kine-matic:** A pun on "kinematic" (relating to motion) and "Kine" for Kubernetes. It suggests automated media creation.
*   **Cine-Gen:** Simple, elegant, and directly combines "Cinema" and "Generator".
*   **KubeDirector:** Evokes the idea of a film director orchestrating the creation process using Kubernetes-like commands.
*   **Media-Forge:** Implies a powerful factory for crafting media assets.

### Clever & Punny
*   **Genie-CTL:** Like a magical genie you control with `ctl` commands (`kubectl`). "I wish for a video of a cat playing a piano!"
*   **AIm-Director:** A pun on "Film Director", emphasizing the AI's role in the creative process.
*   **Kuber-net-o-scope:** A mouthful, but a fun play on "Kinetoscope," one of the earliest motion-picture devices.
*   **Gen-Cine:** A subtle nod to the legendary film critic Gene Siskel.

### Gemini-Centric
*   **Gem-CTL:** Short, sweet, and puts Gemini at the core of the control mechanism.
*   **Kube-Gem:** A simple fusion of Kubernetes and Gemini, suggesting a precious tool.
*   **Gemini-Reels:** Clearly states that Gemini is the one creating the "film reels".
