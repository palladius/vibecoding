# How to Get from Milan to Modena by Train

Getting from Milan to Modena by train is simple and fast, with several direct options available.

## Summary

*   **Direct Trains:** Yes, there are frequent direct trains, so you do not need to change.
*   **Travel Time:** The fastest journey is approximately **1 hour and 15 minutes**. The average time is about 1 hour and 45 minutes.
*   **Operators:** You can choose between Italy's main high-speed train operators:
    *   **Trenitalia** (with their *Frecciarossa* and *Intercity* trains)
    *   **Italo**
*   **Departure Station:** Trains depart from **Milano Centrale**, the main train station in Milan.
*   **Arrival Station:** All trains arrive at **Modena's main station**.
*   **Frequency:** Trains run frequently throughout the day, with roughly 15-20 direct options to choose from, often departing hourly.

## Recommendation

For the best experience, booking a high-speed train (*Frecciarossa* or *Italo*) in advance is recommended. This will give you the fastest travel time and often a better price. You can buy tickets online directly from the Trenitalia or Italo websites or use a comparison site like TheTrainLine.com.

In short: **Yes, there is a direct train. It's fast, convenient, and the recommended way to travel between the two cities.**
